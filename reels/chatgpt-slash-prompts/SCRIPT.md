## How each example is shown

Every one of the 8 examples follows the same 6.5 s interaction inside a ChatGPT app frame (copied from your screenshot: Chat/Work switch, purple **Create image**, `+`, the gauge and mic icons, purple send button, official ChatGPT logo):

1. The **original photo** flies in ("📷 الصورة الأصلية", or "🔁 نفس الصورة" when the photo is reused)
2. The cursor taps **+** and the photo drops into the composer as an attachment
3. The **slash prompt is typed** letter by letter (exact text from `slash-prompt.txt`)
4. Tap on the purple send button, the message moves up into the chat
5. **Creating image**: the result sharpens from top to bottom, like in ChatGPT
6. **Image created** hit (flash + confetti + sound), the result pops out of the chat in 3D with an input pill under it (original photo + slash prompt), and the caption explains it

Every image keeps its real proportions (4:5, 2:3 or 3:2), with no cropping or stretching.

## Accuracy notes (from the asset pack README)

- The slash words are **short prompts the image model interprets, not official ChatGPT commands**. The video says so in the caveat scene ("prompts قصار، ماشي commands رسميين") and shows the optional one-time setup sentence.
- The ChatGPT screen in the video is a **motion-graphic recreation** of the app UI, not a screen recording. The results were generated by sending each photo with the exact text in `slash-prompt.txt`.
- The character sheet uses the exact final text `/characterSheet no text` (the first version invented biographical text and was excluded).
- The ad copy, burger ingredient labels and billboard scene are AI-generated concepts, not real product claims or a real ad placement.
- `/sticker` was generated with a transparent background, which is why it shows on a checkerboard in the chat and floats without a box when it pops out.

## How it's built (to edit and re-render)

- `index.html` holds the whole animation as a deterministic timeline. Open `index.html?t=30` in Chrome to preview any second.
- The 8 examples are the `DEMOS` list near the top of the script (asset number, exact prompt, aspect ratio, Darija caption). Change a caption or the order there.
- `img/` holds resized copies of the originals (`o*`), results (`r*`), thumbnails (`ot*`, `rt*`) and pre-blurred results (`b*`) for the "Creating image" reveal.
- Render: `node render.js cues build/cues.json && python3 audio.py build/cues.json build`, then `node render.js video build/video_noaudio.mp4 30 4 4`, then `bash finish.sh chatgpt-slash-prompts`.
